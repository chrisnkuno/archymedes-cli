- Fork, modify, redistribute and build commercial products on the source code, per the license.
- State factually that your product "is built on", "is based on", "uses" or "is a fork of"

- Keep the name in unmodified files, commit history, and the `NOTICE` file.

- Use the marks on merchandise.

If you distribute a modified build, give it your own name and make clear it is not the
official project. Questions: open an issue.
