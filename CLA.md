## 1. Definitions

- **"You"** means the individual, or the legal entity on whose behalf an authorised individual

  controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with it.
- **"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship, including modifications or additions
  to an existing work, that You intentionally submit to Archymedes for inclusion in a project, in
  any form (pull request, patch, issue attachment, or otherwise).
- **"Submit"** means any form of electronic, verbal, or written communication sent to Archymedes or
  its representatives for the purpose of discussing or improving a project, except communication
  You conspicuously mark "Not a Contribution".

## Part A — Individual

By commenting on a pull request with the exact sentence the CLA check asks for (currently
"I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA"), You agree to this agreement as an
individual for that and all future Contributions. The signature record (GitHub username, the pull
request, and the time) is stored in this repository by the CLA workflow.

## Part B — Entity (corporate)

An entity signs by sending Archymedes, Inc. a copy of this agreement signed by an authorised
representative, listing its name, address, a point of contact, and the GitHub usernames of the
employees authorised to contribute on its behalf (the list may be updated by the point of
contact). Contact the maintainers through a GitHub issue to arrange this. Until it is on file,
employees contributing on the entity's behalf should not sign Part A in its place.
